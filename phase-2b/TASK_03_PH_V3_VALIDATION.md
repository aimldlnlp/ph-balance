# Task 03: PH-V3 Validation

## Validate before experiments.

Check:

## Controller Direction

Verify:

positive theta: - corrective torque

negative theta: - opposite corrective torque

## Position Stabilization

Verify:

positive wheel displacement: - restoring action

## Equilibrium

Check:

theta=0 position=0

should not generate unwanted torque.

## Stability

Perform:

-   local linearization
-   eigenvalue inspection
-   nonlinear simulation sanity check

Do not claim stability without evidence.
